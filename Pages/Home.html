Espresso

A rich and bold shot of pure coffee extracted from finely-ground beans. Served in small portions, it's the foundation for many other coffee drinks.

Cappuccino

A classic Italian drink made with equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam. Perfect for a cozy and balanced coffee experience.

Latte

Made with a shot of espresso and topped with steamed milk and a light layer of foam. Smooth, creamy, and ideal for those who prefer a lighter taste.

Cold Brew

Steeped in cold water for hours, cold brew coffee delivers a smooth, low-acid taste. Served chilled, it's a refreshing twist on traditional coffee.

Mocha

A delicious blend of espresso, chocolate syrup, and steamed milk. It's a favorite for chocolate and coffee lovers alike.

Macchiato

An espresso "stained" with a small amount of milk. Strong, bold, and perfect for those who like their coffee with a touch of creaminess.

Flat White

Similar to a latte but with a higher coffee-to-milk ratio. Smooth, velvety, and rich in flavor — a favorite among espresso lovers.
